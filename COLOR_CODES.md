### Facility UI Color Palette Suggestions

Here is a balanced, high-contrast, modern UI color palette tailored for gacha/RPG facility themes:

---

### 1. Power (Raw Force / Physical Might)
- **Primary HEX:** `#EF4444` *(Vibrant Crimson / Red)*
- **RGB:** `rgb(239, 68, 68)`
- **TextMeshPro Tag:** `<color=#EF4444>Power</color>`
- **Accents:** `#B91C1C` (Dark/Border), `#FCA5A5` (Glow/Highlight)
- **Visual Vibe:** Aggressive, strong, high energy.

---

### 2. Agility (Speed / Reflexes / Wind)
- **Primary HEX:** `#10B981` *(Emerald / Mint Green)*
- **RGB:** `rgb(16, 185, 129)`
- **TextMeshPro Tag:** `<color=#10B981>Agility</color>`
- **Accents:** `#047857` (Dark/Border), `#6EE7B7` (Glow/Highlight)
- **Visual Vibe:** Fast, refreshing, fluid.

---

### 3. Impact (Burst / Explosion / Shock)
- **Primary HEX:** `#F59E0B` *(Warm Amber / Golden Orange)*
- **RGB:** `rgb(245, 158, 11)`
- **TextMeshPro Tag:** `<color=#F59E0B>Impact</color>`
- **Accents:** `#B45309` (Dark/Border), `#FCD34D` (Glow/Highlight)
- **Visual Vibe:** Explosive, striking, high-impact.

---

### 4. Defense (Shield / Resilience / Guard)
- **Primary HEX:** `#3B82F6` *(Cobalt / Royal Blue)*
- **RGB:** `rgb(59, 130, 246)`
- **TextMeshPro Tag:** `<color=#3B82F6>Defense</color>`
- **Accents:** `#1D4ED8` (Dark/Border), `#93C5FD` (Glow/Highlight)
- **Visual Vibe:** Solid, dependable, fortified.

---

### 5. Finesse (Technique / Precision / Mastery)
- **Primary HEX:** `#8B5CF6` *(Amethyst / Bright Violet)*
- **RGB:** `rgb(139, 92, 246)`
- **TextMeshPro Tag:** `<color=#8B5CF6>Finesse</color>`
- **Accents:** `#6D28D9` (Dark/Border), `#C4B5FD` (Glow/Highlight)
- **Visual Vibe:** Elegant, sharp, technical, mysterious.

---
